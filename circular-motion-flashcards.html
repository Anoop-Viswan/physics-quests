How it works

Answer in your head, then flip

  • Say your answer out loud (or write it), then tap the card to flip it.
  • Rate yourself honestly. Again = the card comes back in a few cards. Almost = it comes back later. Got it = done for today, and tomorrow it shows up after the harder ones.
  • The deck saves on this device. Over a few days, the hard cards rise to the top and the easy ones fade.
