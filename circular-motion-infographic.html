Circular Motion:
The Big Idea

Going around a circle means you are always turning, so you are always accelerating, even at a steady speed.

Let go! Which way does it fly?

No string = no force = straight line (Newton's 1st law), along the tangent. Never straight outward.

"Centrifugal force" is a myth

The car turns, but your body wants to keep going straight. The door runs into you and pushes you inward. Nothing pushes you out.
