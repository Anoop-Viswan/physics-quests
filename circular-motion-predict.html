Sim missions · 20–30 min · after the challenges

Break the physics yourself

Free simulators, no account needed. Each mission is predict-then-test again: write the guess down before you click.

Mission 1 · PhET "Gravity and Orbits": switch gravity off

  1. Open the sim → Model. Tick Velocity and Gravity Force, and press play.
  2. Predict: if you untick Gravity mid-orbit, which way does the planet go? Now test it. (Same idea as Challenge 1.)
  3. Predict: how do the velocity and gravity arrows sit relative to each other? Check: they stay at 90° in a circular orbit.
  4. Predict: double the star's mass. Does the planet stay in a circle? Why not? (Hint: GM/r² went up, but v²/r didn't.)

Mission 2 · Physics Classroom Concept Builders: the direction game

  1. Circular Logic: pick the directions of v, a and Fnet until it says you've mastered it. It keeps giving you questions until you're consistent, so it works like a game with levels.
  2. Forces and FBDs in Circular Motion: the FBD drill for loops, hills and curves.
  3. Choose Guest (you don't need a login). Progress isn't saved, so finish one in a sitting (about 10–15 min each).

Mission 3 · Physics Classroom Interactive: design a roller coaster

  1. Roller Coaster Design: change the loop and hill sizes and watch the g-forces on the rider.
  2. Predict first: where will the rider feel heaviest? Lightest? Then test whether a smaller loop radius at the top makes things better or worse. (Look back at the 1895 Flip Flap story in the Quest.)
